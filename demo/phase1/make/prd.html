Nakhla Rewards: Sign-up and Account

Product Requirements Document, version 0.9 (draft). Owner: Product team. Fictional demo document.

1. Purpose

Nakhla Rewards is the loyalty app of the Nakhla coffee chain in Saudi Arabia. Customers collect points on every purchase and redeem them for drinks. This document covers creating an account, verifying the mobile number and the first login.

2. Users

New customers in Saudi Arabia who are 18 years or older. Staff accounts are created by admins in the back office and are out of scope for the customer app.

3. Sign-up form

IDRule
R1Full name is required.
R2Mobile number is required and must be a valid Saudi mobile number. It is the login identifier.
R3Email is optional. If given, the customer receives a welcome email.
R4Password must be at least 8 characters.
R5Date of birth is required; customers must be 18 or older.
R6The customer chooses Arabic or English. The app supports both languages.
R7The customer must accept the Terms and Conditions before signing up.
R8A mobile number that is already registered cannot be used to sign up again.

4. Mobile verification

IDRule
R9After the form is submitted, a one-time password (OTP) is sent to the mobile number by SMS.
R10If the customer enters a wrong OTP, an error is shown.
R11The customer can ask for a new OTP.
R12When the number is verified, the account is created and the customer is logged in.

5. Welcome points

R13. Every new member receives 50 welcome points.

6. Login and security

R14. Customers log in with mobile number and password.

R15. The account is locked after several failed login attempts.

R16. Personal data is stored securely.

7. Quality

R17. Registration must be fast.

R18. The sign-up screens must be user-friendly.

8. Out of scope

Payments, redeeming points, and the staff back office.
